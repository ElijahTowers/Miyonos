MIYONOS WI-FI INSTALLER FOR MACOS

1. Turn on the Miyoo Mini Plus and connect it to the same trusted Wi-Fi as
   this Mac.
2. On the Miyoo, open Apps > Tweaks > Network > SSH and enable SSH.
3. Note the IP address shown at the top of that screen.
4. Double-click "Open Miyonos Installer.command".
5. Enter the IP address in the browser page and click "Install or update".

The default OnionOS account is onion with password onion. The browser page is
available only on this Mac. The password is not stored or sent over the
internet.

Keep all files in this folder together. The same button installs future
updates. Your settings, logs, and artwork will be preserved.
